This post is the first in a series about the Library of Congress AI Sandbox for staff. It provides an introduction to the initiative’s goals and an overview of the types of experiments conducted so far. Future posts will feature details about experiments in text recognition and searchability, metadata and bibliographic record enhancement, and more.
