The Local History and Genealogy Web Archive preserves websites that help researchers trace family histories and better understand the communities where their ancestors lived. In this interview, Ahmed Johnson discusses the importance of preserving the websites of local history and genealogical societies, the challenges of building a broadly representative collection, and the role of web archiving in connecting future researchers with the past.
