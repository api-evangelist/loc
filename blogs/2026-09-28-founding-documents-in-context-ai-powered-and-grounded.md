This post describes a recent collaboration between the Digital Strategy Directorate in the Library’s Office of the Chief Information Officer and the Arizona State University Cloud Innovation Center. The partnership explored necessary steps to create an effective AI-powered tool using context engineering allows for the exploration of United States Revolutionary Era history with the Library’s collections as grounding data.
